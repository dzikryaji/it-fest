# 🐾 Paws & Focus  

🚀 **The Ultimate Focus Companion** 🚀  

Paws & Focus is an **Android productivity app** designed to help users **stay on track, manage their time effectively, and boost their focus**. Built with **Pomodoro techniques, leaderboards, note-taking, and AI-driven focus counseling**, this app gamifies productivity, making studying and working more engaging.  

## ✨ Features  
✅ **Focus Timer** – Track your study and work sessions with an intuitive timer.  
✅ **Personal Notes** – Organize and save your thoughts efficiently.  
✅ **Leaderboard** – Compete with others by racking up focus hours.  
✅ **AI-Powered Focus Counseling** – Get smart productivity tips powered by **Google Gemini API**.  
✅ **Gamification** – Stay motivated with engaging challenges and progress tracking.  

## 🛠 Tech Stack  
- **Kotlin** – Efficient and modern Android development.  
- **MVVM Architecture** – Clean code structure for better maintainability.  
- **Firebase** – Real-time data management and synchronization.  
- **Google Gemini API** – AI-powered productivity guidance.  

## 🎉 Achievements  
Paws & Focus was a **finalist** in the **Hackathon IT Fest Micro IPB 2024**, showcasing its impact in the productivity space!
